Questions

Fair questions,
straight answers

How much should I budget for ad spend?

Most local businesses see meaningful results starting around $500–$1,500/mo, paid directly to Meta and Google from your own accounts. On the audit call we'll recommend a number based on your services, prices and area — and we'll tell you honestly if ads aren't the right first move.

Is the makeover really free?

Yes. It's a one-time overhaul included when you start the Local Ads Engine — we won't send paid traffic to profiles that undersell you. Everything we fix is yours to keep, even if you cancel after the first month.

Will the AI sound like a robot?

No — it's trained on your services, prices, hours and the way you actually talk to clients. It also knows its limits: anything unusual gets handed to a human right away, and you get full transcripts of every conversation.

I already have a booking system. Does that work?

Perfect — we connect to what you have. Square, GlossGenius, Vagaro, Fresha, Jane, Calendly, Housecall Pro and most others. If you don't have one, we'll set one up as part of the makeover.

How fast will I see results?

The makeover and AI receptionist start working in week one — missed calls stop slipping away immediately. Ads typically take 2–4 weeks to find their footing as we test offers and audiences, then improve from there.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Everything is month-to-month. If we're not clearly paying for ourselves, you should fire us — and it takes one email to do it.

Who owns the ad accounts and pages?

You do — always. We build inside your accounts, so every campaign, audience, review and follower stays with you no matter what.
